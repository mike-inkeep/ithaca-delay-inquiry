Class B — testimony on file

  • Penelope — selective, formidable, counsel present.
  • Telemachus — full cooperation; includes the Pylos/Sparta interview notes.
  • Circe — cooperative; affidavit signed with small pig drawing (uninterpreted).
  • Calypso — conflicted; paperwork immaculate.
  • Polyphemus — hostile; statement delivered ballistically; fully corroborated.
  • The Phaeacian court stenographer — transcript of the keynote, complete, including the pauses for gifts.

Class C — the subject's account

Books 9–12. Stored in a reinforced cabinet, not because the document is dangerous, but because the Committee's clerks kept reading it for pleasure, which is precisely how it works. See The Phaeacian Speaking Tour for the performance conditions under which it was minted.

Exhibits, scorched

A dedicated fire-adjacent shelf holds submissions read aloud within range of Penelope or her proxy panel:

  1. The full text of The Home Renovation Avoidance Hypothesis (first draft, margins annotated "bold").
  2. The The Sirens' Encore Clause staging diagram, with seat assignment.
  3. A junior clerk's memo titled "Was the loom, itself, a delay? Asking for the Committee." (The clerk is fine. The memo is not.)

Missing / wanted

  • The Redacted Testimony of Eurylochus — the second-in-command's account of Aeaea, the strait, and Thrinacia. The single document most likely to re-grade half the Theory Catalog. Lost with the fleet; the Committee's standing reward (one unscorched exhibit of the claimant's choosing) remains unclaimed.
  • Ogygia's land registry — to test the timeshare theory's boldest speculation (The Ogygia Timeshare Presentation).
  • Any log, from any ship, for the stretch between the strait and Thrinacia — see The Strait Insurance Dispute for who benefits from the gap.

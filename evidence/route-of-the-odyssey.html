Route of the Odyssey

Reading the graph

  • The two thick nodes are the case. Aeaea (1 year) and Ogygia (7 years) between them absorb 80% of the decade — see Delay Attribution for the wedge chart. Every hazard node on the graph combined is a rounding error by comparison (the timeline's finding).
  • The dashed loop at the top is the Inquiry's founding wound. Nine days of perfect sailing to within sight of home, undone in one nap (The Aeolus Baggage Incident). Every serious theory must metabolize this loop: the road home demonstrably worked when pointed the right way.
  • The double-line edge at the bottom is the closing argument. From Scheria, home was one chauffeured night. The distance was never the variable. See The Phaeacian Speaking Tour.

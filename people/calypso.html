Calypso

Goddess. Sole proprietor and, the Committee believes, sole resident agent of Ogygia.

"I welcomed him, I fed him, I promised to make him immortal and ageless all his days."
— Calypso's own statement, which she filed believing it was exculpatory

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Venue Ogygia (beachfront cave, cedar fires, four springs, vine-draped)
Guests on record, ever 1
Engagement length 7 years (2,555 days — 70% of the missing decade)
Offer extended Immortality + agelessness + the property
Offer status Declined after 2,555 days of "thinking about it"
Exit logistics Axe, timber access, provisions, favorable wind — 4 days

The seven-year question

Calypso's file contains the Inquiry's central asymmetry:

  • Her account: she loved him, she kept him, she was ordered by Olympus to release him, and she complied under protest — with, she notes, better severance than any god ever offered a mortal.
  • His account: captivity, nightly weeping on the shore, an unwilling bed.
  • The record's account: no walls, no guards, raft-grade timber on-site for seven years, and an exit that took four days once anyone decided to exit.

The Ogygia Timeshare Presentation resolves the asymmetry commercially: an open-ended pitch, indefinitely deferred. The Grand Unified Theory of Heroic Procrastination resolves it psychologically: the pitch was himself, unchanged, forever — the one offer he could neither accept nor stop listening to. Calypso has read both files and endorsed, tartly, "whichever one makes him look worse."

The double-standard memorandum

The Inquiry preserves, verbatim in spirit, Calypso's objection on release: the gods permit themselves mortal lovers and begrudge every goddess hers. The Committee — convened, after all, to audit excuses — certifies this as the only statement in the entire file that is simultaneously (a) self-serving and (b) true. It has been framed and hung in the Committee's break room, over the objection of Hermes.

Assessment

As a witness: conflicted but load-bearing. She is the only living source for seven-tenths of the delay, her interests are adverse to every party including herself, and her physical evidence (the raft manifest, the four-day timeline) has checked out completely. The Committee's standing note: when the goddess's paperwork is cleaner than the hero's, audit the hero.

Poseidon

God of the sea, earthquakes, and horses. Father of Polyphemus. Respondent (in absentia) to all Inquiry correspondence.

"Grant that Odysseus may never reach his home — or if it is fated that he must, let him come late, in misery, on a stranger's ship, having lost all his companions."
— The grudge, as filed by Polyphemus and executed by his father to the letter

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Jurisdiction The entire route (inconveniently)
Grievance Blinding of son; aggravated by name-and-address mockery
Instruments Storms, adverse winds, one raft demolition
Requests for comment 11 sent; 11 returned water-damaged

The precision problem

The Committee's actuaries have studied the grudge-as-executed and found it suspiciously precise. The prayer requested: late arrival, misery, a stranger's ship, total crew loss. The outcome: ten years late, miserable (per the subject; see source caveats), delivered by Phaeacian ferry (The Phaeacian Speaking Tour), crew lost to the last man.

Here is what the actuaries flag: Poseidon never actually blocked the road. At every fatal juncture, the proximate cause was human — the bag opened by the crew (The Aeolus Baggage Incident), the cattle eaten by the crew (The Thrinacian Catering Invoice), the seven years of "deliberation" (The Ogygia Timeshare Presentation). The sea god supplied weather; the itinerary supplied itself. A grudge that only ever needed to underwrite the subject's own decisions is either divinely economical or — as The Grand Unified Theory of Heroic Procrastination argues — the most over-credited line item in the ledger.

The one direct action

The record shows exactly one personally-delivered Poseidon intervention: the raft demolition off Scheria, upon returning from a vacation (the Ethiopians; the god's own itinerary is not the Inquiry's business) to find the prospect nearly home. One storm, professionally executed, non-fatal by his own choice of margin. Even at maximum wrath, the Committee notes, he delivered the subject to the best xenia in the Mediterranean. The grudge's terms required arrival — late, miserable, alone — and its executor kept to spec with the discipline of a god who reads contracts.

Assessment

As an explanation: a weather system with a motive — necessary to the official account, sufficient for none of it. The Inquiry's position (methodology): "Poseidon was angry" explains the sea state, never the seven years on a beach with catering. As a witness: unavailable; all service of process returns with kelp.

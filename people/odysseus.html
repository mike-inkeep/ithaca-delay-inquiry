Odysseus

King of Ithaca. Veteran of Troy (10 years). Traveller (10 more). Narrator (unfortunately).

"He saw the cities of many men and knew their minds."
— The tradition. The Inquiry adds: and their guest rooms.

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Epithet Polytropos ("of many turns" — see disputed translation)
Distance from Troy to home ~565 nautical miles
Reasonable transit time ~3 weeks
Actual transit time 10 years
Overrun ~17,000%
Days personally vouched for by independent witnesses 4 years' worth
Days vouched for solely by the subject the rest

Assessment

The Inquiry's difficulty is not that the subject lies. It is that he lies well, on the record, with craftsman's pride, and then occasionally tells a truth so verifiable (The Aeolus Baggage Incident) that the whole instrument must be recalibrated.

He is the only source for: the Lotus-Eaters' duration, the Underworld agenda, the Sirens' setlist, the strait casualty sequence, the Thrinacian wind report, and the emotional weather on Ogygia. Every one of those claims was first uttered at a paid engagement (The Phaeacian Speaking Tour). The Committee does not say this makes the claims false. The Committee says it makes them content.

Documented competencies

  • Deception, tactical: the horse; "Nobody" (The 'Nobody' Personal Brand Crisis); the beggar audit of his own house.
  • Deception, recreational: lied to Athena within minutes of landing home, for practice, while she was disguised and grading him. She gave notes. He kept the notes.
  • Carpentry: one raft (seaworthy, 4 days); one marriage bed (olive tree, load-bearing — see the bed test).
  • Punctuality: no documented instances.

Relations

  • Spouse: Penelope — see her file for the twenty-year mirror-image strategy.
  • Son: Telemachus — met as an infant, re-met as a co-combatant.
  • Patron: Athena (declined deposition).
  • Standing enemy: Poseidon — self-inflicted; see the address-shouting incident.
  • Hosts of record: Calypso, Circe, Aeolus, the court of Scheria.

The Committee's one sincere paragraph

Ten years at war, ten years at sea, and the man's first act on his own beach was to lie about his name to a stranger. The Inquiry maintains its files, its ledgers, and its rubric — but it concedes, in this one paragraph and nowhere else, that a person can become so good at the journey that the arrival is the only thing left to fear. The paragraph is over. See The Grand Unified Theory of Heroic Procrastination for the billable version.

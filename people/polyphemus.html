Polyphemus

Cyclops. Shepherd. Son of Poseidon. Complainant.

"Nobody is killing me by trickery, Nobody by force!"
— The complainant, executing the perpetrator's script, verbatim, to his own rescuers

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Residence Cave, unnamed island; single-occupancy; excellent cheese operation
Injury Total (the eye)
Property loss Multiple sheep, several guests' worth of wine
Personnel loss inflicted 6 of the subject's crew (documented; method: unspeakable)
Statement to the Inquiry Given; hostile; thrown (the statement was a boulder)

The incident, both accounts

The subject's account (as performed): a monster imprisoned peaceful visitors and ate six of them; the survivors improvised heroically — wine, the "Nobody" alias, the stake, the sheep exfiltration.

The complainant's account (reconstructed): twenty-three armed men entered a private residence uninvited, ate the food stores, hid, and when the owner returned and — the complainant concedes this point poorly — ate several of them, they blinded him, mocked him from their ship, and left him a name and address he could do nothing with except pray. Which, he notes, worked.

The Committee scores the ethics a draw and the aftermath decisively: see The 'Nobody' Personal Brand Crisis for the escalation ladder that turned a clean escape into a decade of Poseidon-sponsored weather.

Why his testimony matters

Polyphemus is the Inquiry's only hostile witness whose every checkable claim has checked:

  • The blinding: confirmed (universally; he is very visibly blinded).
  • The "Nobody" exchange: confirmed by the perpetrator, proudly, at a paid engagement.
  • The prayer to his father: confirmed by ten years of subsequent marine weather (Delay Attribution itemizes the storms).
  • The prophecy: a seer had told him a man named Odysseus would take his eye. His only complaint on this point — that he'd been expecting someone taller — is the record's sole surviving physical description of the subject, and the Committee treasures it.

Assessment

As a witness: hostile, monocular, and consistently corroborated — the exact inverse of the subject's profile, a symmetry the Committee's statisticians refuse to call a coincidence. His standing request (that the Inquiry's findings be read to him aloud, slowly, near the shore, so he can throw things at the reader) has been accommodated once, by the junior clerk, who is fine.

Circe

Goddess, pharmacological practitioner, and proprietor of the Aeaea estate.

"They stayed with her a full year, feasting on abundant meat and sweet wine."
— The record, on the engagement she still describes as "a normal length residency"

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Venue Aeaea (forest estate; wolves and lions, unusually agreeable)
Engagement length 1 year + 1 encore night
Guests transformed 22 (restored; upgraded, per the guests, "taller and handsomer than before")
Guests not transformed 1 (Eurylochus; ate nothing; see Redacted Testimony)
After-care provided Full route brief, monster-by-monster, plus the Underworld protocol

The transformation matter

The pig incident is addressed at length in the residency file. Circe's position, notarized: the menu's effects were disclosed in the footer, the restorations were prompt once requested through proper channels (a man with a magic herb and a drawn sword constitutes proper channels), and the guests left in better condition than they arrived, a warranty no other host in the record offers.

The Committee observes that both facts can be true: the year was consensual and it began with twenty-two men eating an insufficiently-read menu.

The after-care record

Whatever the Inquiry concludes about the year, Circe's exit package is the gold standard in the file:

  • Complete routing: Sirens (wax protocol), the strait (expected-loss figure — see The Strait Insurance Dispute for whose problem that number became), Thrinacia (the cattle clause, in writing).
  • The Underworld consultation protocol, including which prophet, which rites, and what the dead drink.
  • On the encore visit (the return leg): burial support for Elpenor and a second full briefing.

No other host briefed the road ahead. Calypso provided a raft and a wave. Aeolus provided the weather in a bag and then unlisted his island. Circe provided documentation, and the Committee — an institution made entirely of documentation — has never fully recovered.

Assessment

As a witness: cooperative to a degree the Committee found suspicious until it checked out. Every verifiable Circe claim has verified. Her only contested assertion is the duration's characterization ("residency" vs. "a year nobody was counting"), and on that point she has submitted the killing detail herself: the crew had to remind the subject to leave. She signed the affidavit with a small drawing of a pig. The Committee has chosen not to interpret it.

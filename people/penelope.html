Penelope

Queen of Ithaca. Spouse of the subject. The Inquiry's control group.

"So by day she would weave at the great web, and by night unravel it."
— The record, describing the only delay in this entire Inquiry executed competently

Why her file matters

The Inquiry studies a man who took ten years to come home. It would be methodologically negligent not to study the woman who, over the same window plus a decade, held a besieged position using nothing but process.

While the subject's delays required goddesses, monsters, and weather systems, Penelope's required: one loom, one shroud, 108 hostile stakeholders, and a nightly maintenance window. Her delaying action ran three years before detection — a duration that beats every uncorroborated segment of her husband's itinerary except the ones with goddesses in them, and did so in her own house, under continuous observation.

The shroud protocol

Phase Action Cover story
Day Weave Laertes' burial shroud, publicly "I will choose a suitor when it is finished"
Night Unweave the day's work None required (the suitors did not think to audit the loom)
Detection (year 3) A maid leaked the protocol Renegotiated under duress; finished the shroud; kept delaying anyway

The Committee has cross-filed the protocol under the Loom Question and notes that The Home Renovation Avoidance Hypothesis attempts, bravely, to read it as a change-order system. Counsel for Penelope has responded with a single raised eyebrow, now in the Evidence Locker.

The bed test

On the traveller's return, Penelope did not run the standard verification (scars, memories, dog). She ran an adversarial probe: she ordered the marriage bed moved — a bed she knew to be carved into a living olive tree and therefore immovable. The disguised subject's outraged, carpentry-grade objection was the authentication. The Inquiry regards this as the finest single interrogation in the record and has adopted it as a rubric axis ("Survives Penelope").

Assessment

Every theory in the Theory Catalog is graded against her — literally: the Survives Penelope axis asks whether an excuse could be stated to this woman's face. The current scoreboard high is 5 (The Aeolus Baggage Incident — externally verified, crew's fault, no goddess involved). The low is 0 (The Home Renovation Avoidance Hypothesis — see the scorched-exhibits shelf).

The Committee's finding: had the couple's competencies been pooled — his improvisation, her process discipline — the return trip would have taken eleven days. Filed under conclusions, uncomfortable.

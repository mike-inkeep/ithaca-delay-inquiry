Witnesses, unavailable

  • Eurylochus — second-in-command; on-record dissenter at Aeaea and Thrinacia; his Redacted Testimony is the Inquiry's most-wanted document. Lost with the fleet.
  • Elpenor — youngest crewman; fell off Circe's roof during the abrupt departure (see the tempo problem). Interviewed once, posthumously, mostly about funeral logistics.
  • The three lotus scouts — see The Lotus-Eaters Wellness Retreat. Present-tense to this day, presumably.
  • The crew (all) — 600+ at sailing, 0 at arrival. The Inquiry notes that every hospitality overrun was survived and every transit was lethal, a statistic the hospitality theories cite constantly.

Divine observers (declined to be deposed)

  • Athena — the subject's patron; materially accelerated the final approach while having watched the preceding decade with, the record suggests, a clipboard. The Committee's subpoena was returned transformed into an owl pellet.
  • Hermes — served process twice (Ogygia exit notice; Aeaean antidote delivery). The only god with a clean paper trail.
  • Zeus — final arbiter of both the Helios settlement and the closing ceasefire. Responds to all inquiries with weather.

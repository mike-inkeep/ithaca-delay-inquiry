Aeaea

Forest island estate. Proprietor: Circe. Venue of the one-year engagement.

"They found the house of Circe, built of polished stone, in a place with wide views — and around it, wolves and mountain lions, fawning."
— The site survey. The predators are tame. The Inquiry files that under amenity and disclosure, simultaneously.

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Structure Polished-stone house, elevated site, professional herb garden
Fauna Wolves and lions (transformed prior guests, per deposition; "rescues," per proprietor)
Kitchen The finest in the record — see The Aeaean Culinary Residency
Stated stay 1 week
Actual stay 1 year + 1 encore night
Exit package Full route documentation, monster-by-monster (unmatched; see after-care)

The estate's two reputations

Aeaea enters the tradition as a horror venue (men into pigs) and exits it as the only place that ever helped the mission (the route brief, the Underworld protocol, the strait's expected-loss figure). The Inquiry holds both to be accurate and notes the pivot happened at approximately the moment the proprietor and the subject reached what the record calls, with unusual delicacy, an understanding.

The venue's true significance for the delay file is the exit dynamics: at year's end, the record states plainly, the crew had to convene a delegation to remind their commander that home existed. The estate had no enchantment active at that point — the proprietor confirms, the crew confirms, the menus were merely excellent. It is the cleanest documented instance of the file's core phenomenon: the amenities doing what no monster could. See Exhibit E.

Repeat business

Aeaea is the only venue in the Gazetteer with a documented return visit — the post-Underworld stopover for Elpenor's burial and one more night of catering (the audit's view). The proprietor used the encore to deliver a second full route briefing. The Committee's hospitality analysts rate this "customer success behavior a millennium ahead of schedule."

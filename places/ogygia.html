Ogygia

Remote island. Sole proprietor: Calypso. Venue of 70% of the missing decade.

"Even a god who came there would gaze in wonder, and be glad in heart."
— The record's site survey. Note that even the hostile account concedes the venue.

Inquiry profile

Field Value
Location Far from everything, by design ("the navel of the sea")
Amenities Cedar and juniper fires, four springs, vine-heavy cave, meadows of violet and parsley
Walls / guards / gates None / none / none
Raft-grade timber on site Alder, poplar, pine — standing, abundant, seven years
Guests, all time 1
Average length of stay 2,555 days (n=1)

The venue as evidence

Ogygia's physical facts are the quiet star witness of The Ogygia Timeshare Presentation:

  • The timber stood the whole time. The raft the subject eventually built took four days with tools the proprietor handed over on request. Every material and skill required for departure existed on-site from day one. What was missing for seven years was the request.
  • The isolation ran both ways. No ships called; but also, no walls held. The record's "captivity" was enforced by geography exactly as much as the subject's daily shore-weeping was witnessed by anyone but the proprietor and, later, the subject's own keynote (The Phaeacian Speaking Tour).
  • The amenities were divine-grade. The site survey above was filed by Hermes, a witness with no stake and a documented preference for getting in and out of places quickly. When the process server lingers to admire the meadow, the venue is material to the case.

The exit, timed

Day Event
0 Hermes delivers the release order; Calypso objects (see the memorandum), complies
1–4 Raft built (axe, auger, timber, sail-cloth provided)
5 Provisions loaded: bread, water, wine, a warm following wind
5 Departure. After 2,555 days, the venue's off-boarding took five.

The Committee prints this table wherever the word "captivity" appears in submissions.

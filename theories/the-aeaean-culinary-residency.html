The Aeaean Culinary Residency

"They stayed with her a full year, feasting on abundant meat and sweet wine."
— The official account, in a rare moment of not even trying to hide it

Status: Leading theory. Absorbs 365 of the 3,650 missing days (10%), with witnesses.

The Theory

The year on Aeaea was not enchantment. It was a residency. Circe — a niche practitioner with an isolated venue, a herb garden of professional grade, and a documented interest in transformation — hosted the crew of Ithaca's flagship for a twelve-month engagement of what the record itself describes as daily feasting.

The theory's central claim: nobody was tricked. The crew's own testimony (Class B, unusually consistent) is that at the end of the year the crew had to remind Odysseus it was time to leave. This is not the behavior of prisoners. This is the behavior of a party whose lead guest has been comped into complacency — the pattern that recurs, at 7× the scale, on Ogygia (The Ogygia Timeshare Presentation).

The pig incident

The theory must address the pigs. Its account:

  1. The advance party arrived unannounced, twenty-three men, at lunch service.
  2. They were seated anyway (see xenia) and served the tasting menu.
  3. The menu's first course transformed the diner's relationship with food. In twenty-two documented cases, it transformed slightly more than that.
  4. Eurylochus, who waited outside and ate nothing, was not transformed — which the Committee notes is exactly how a menu works.

Circe has provided notarized menus for the relevant service. The transformation clause appears in the footer, in Linear B, as required by the regulations of the era. Her full statement is on file at her page. The missing corroboration is the Redacted Testimony of Eurylochus, lost since antiquity.

Supporting Evidence

  • The witnesses agree. Uniquely in this inquiry, an entire surviving crew corroborates the duration and the catering. No crew member has ever filed a complaint about the year itself. Several filed complaints about leaving.
  • The exit interview included a full itinerary. Circe provided turn-by-turn sailing directions, monster-by-monster, including which crew would not survive (The Strait Insurance Dispute disputes her liability posture). Practitioners do not provide this level of after-care to prisoners.
  • The return visit. After the Underworld errand (The Underworld Networking Summit), the crew sailed back to Aeaea for one more night. You do not return to your captor for the dessert course. You return for the dessert course.

Problems

  • A year is long, even for tasting menus. The longest documented residency of the era ran a season. The theory requires either an unprecedented engagement or an unadmitted romance, and the record supports — awkwardly — both.
  • Survives-Penelope score: 2. "It was a professional engagement" survives right up until the word "notarized."

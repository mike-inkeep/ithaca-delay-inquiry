The Sirens' Encore Clause

"Untie me! — and he commanded them with his brows, but they bent to their oars and rowed on."
— The record. He asked. To stay. At the Sirens.

Status: Active, tiny, philosophically load-bearing. Absorbs 1 day.

The Theory

The standard reading of the Sirens episode is risk management: wax for the crew, rope for the captain, science achieved safely. The theory before the Committee reads the same facts as a concert booking.

Consider what the subject actually arranged: the only man aboard permitted to hear the performance, in a dedicated seat (the mast), with a contractual guarantee that his requests for an encore be ignored. He did not neutralize the Sirens. He engineered the world's safest front-row ticket and made his crew the venue security.

And when the music played, the record is explicit: he demanded to stay. The rope is the only reason this wiki's subject line isn't "The Sirens' Permanent Residency."

What the Sirens actually sing

The detail the tradition undersells: the Sirens' setlist is not seduction in the abstract. They sing "everything that happened at Troy" — they offer the listener his own story, told perfectly, forever.

The theory's central claim follows: the Sirens are the pure form of every delay in this Inquiry. Calypso offered him immortality; Circe offered him comfort; Scheria offered him an audience (The Phaeacian Speaking Tour); the Sirens offered him himself, as content. He demanded the encore. He always demands the encore. The other eleven files in the Theory Catalog are this file with better catering.

Supporting Evidence

  • The staging is his. Circe provided the warning and the wax protocol. The tied-to-the-mast-hearing arrangement — the part that converts a hazard into a ticket — was the subject's own amendment.
  • The request to stay is on the record. Preserved in his own telling. Even the subject could not resist reporting that he could not resist. (Class C source behaving exactly per profile.)
  • Precedent. He wept at the Phaeacian court when the bard sang of Troy — then commissioned more Troy material (The Phaeacian Speaking Tour). A man who tips the orchestra for the song that makes him cry has a relationship with his own myth that this Committee is not qualified to bill for.

Problems

  • One day. The theory absorbs a single afternoon. As a delay explanation it is negligible; as a diagnosis it is the skeleton key to the whole file cabinet, which is why The Grand Unified Theory of Heroic Procrastination cites it fourteen times.
  • Survives-Penelope score: 1. "I arranged to be tied up so I could safely listen to two women sing about how great I am" has been read aloud to a Penelope proxy panel. The panel's ruling is preserved in the Evidence Locker under exhibits, scorched.
